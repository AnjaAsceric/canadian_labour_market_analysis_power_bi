Canadian Labour Market Analysis — Power BI
An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing Canadian employment, unemployment, wages, industries, demographics, provinces, and Census Metropolitan Areas from 2021 to 2025.
Key insights
National unemployment decreased by 1.08 percentage points between 2021 and 2025.
Average hourly wage increased by $4.83, reaching $29.46 in 2025.
Provincial unemployment trends varied considerably.
Wage patterns differed by age, gender, and industry.
Brantford had the highest unemployment rate among the top five CMAs shown in 2025, at 14.10%.
